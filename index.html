Still Out There

How it was made

The gallery
Built in Three.js as a first-person museum. Distance from each exhibit triggers its story, and a CSS 3D version keeps the walkthrough available on devices without WebGL.
The exhibits
The Apollo objects were reconstructed from archival photographs. The Opportunity, InSight, and Voyager models were adapted from NASA assets for the browser.
The narration
Mission records were adapted into first-person scripts, voiced with Kokoro Heart, and paired with timed captions. Voyager’s activity plays NASA’s plasma-wave data as audio.

How to explore

Move
Use W A S D or the arrow keys to walk.
Hold the on-screen arrow buttons to walk.
Look around
Drag the room to look around, or click it once and move the mouse. Press Esc to free the cursor. Q and E turn left and right.
Drag the room with one finger to look around.
Hear a story
Walk up to an exhibit to start its narration. Use Exhibits to go straight to any artifact.
